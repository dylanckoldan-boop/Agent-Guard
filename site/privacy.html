Privacy policy

Nothing about you leaves your machine

Agent Guard is a local tool and this is a static website. Neither collects anything about you. This page says so plainly.

The application

  • No telemetry. Agent Guard does not send usage data, crash reports or anything else to us or to any third party. It has no analytics.
  • No account, no cloud. There is nothing to sign in to. Your agents, detections and settings never leave your computer.
  • On-device storage only. Agent Guard's signing and quarantine keys, its settings, its tamper-evident audit and any quarantined files live in your own per-user data directory, readable only by you. They are never uploaded.
  • Containment stays inside your perimeter. Agent Guard acts only on resources you own — it never reaches into an agent's own infrastructure.
  • Open source. Agent Guard is MIT licensed, so you can read exactly what it does and verify these claims for yourself.

This website

  • No JavaScript, no cookies. The site is plain HTML and CSS. It sets no cookies and runs no scripts.
  • No third-party requests. Fonts are your system's own and nothing is loaded from other servers, so no outside party sees your visit.
  • No analytics or tracking. We do not log, profile or measure visitors. The only record of your visit is whatever your hosting provider keeps in standard server logs (the site is published with GitHub Pages).

Contact

Questions, or a security concern? Open an issue or use private vulnerability reporting on the project repository.

This policy is dated 26 October 2026. If its meaning ever changes, this date changes with it.
