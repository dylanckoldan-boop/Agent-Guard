What Foldward can do on your computer

  • It can suspend, throttle, quarantine, revoke and terminate programs on the computer it runs on, as part of containing an agent you flag. Quarantine moves a file into an encrypted store and can be restored.
  • You decide what it acts on. By default it reports and recommends. The one automatic action is containing an unregistered agent that opens a canary file — a setting you can turn off — and the ladder stops at “suspend” unless you raise it.
  • It acts only within your own perimeter — resources you own or administer — and never reaches into another party's systems.
  • Use it only on computers you own or are authorised to administer. Running containment on a machine you don't control may be unlawful; that is your responsibility, not the software's.
  • It is not a certified safety system. It is v0.x research software; see what it can't do yet.
