Accessibility statement

Usable by everyone

We want this website and the Foldward app to work for everyone, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard only, zoom or high contrast. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

This website

  • Plain, structured pages. Plain HTML and CSS with headings, landmarks and a “Skip to content” link; nothing depends on JavaScript.
  • Readable contrast. Text and links meet a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 in both the light and the dark theme; an automated test checks the link colours on every change.
  • Your settings win. The pages follow your system's light or dark preference and your browser's text size, and reflow to a single column on narrow screens.
  • Pictures have text. Decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers, and the diagrams have text descriptions.

The desktop app

The app is built with Qt, which works with the screen readers and keyboard navigation of Windows, macOS and Linux. It hasn't had an accessibility review yet.

Known limits

  • Neither the website nor the app has had an independent accessibility audit.
  • Colour marks an agent's state in the app; the state is also written in words in the same row.

Tell us about a barrier

If something is hard to use, open an issue on GitHub and say which page or screen it was and what got in the way.

This statement is dated 31 October 2026.
